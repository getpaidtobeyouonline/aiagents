Section 1 — The Concept

Most online entrepreneurs spend hours every week on tasks that could quite happily run themselves.

Creating content from a blank page every Sunday evening. Copying leads into a spreadsheet by hand, one name at a time. Re-explaining your business to Claude at the start of every single session, because it has no memory of the last one.

This course solves all of that using three free platforms.

You do not need to be technical. You do not need to code. There is no software to buy and nothing to install.

What you need is one afternoon per module, the patience to follow the steps in order, and a willingness to let your tools do the repetitive work for you instead of doing it yourself at eleven at night.

Section 2 — The Breakdown

What an AI agent actually is

An agent is not a single prompt. It is a connected sequence, where the output of each step automatically feeds the next input.

You ask once. Claude works through every step in order and produces a finished result rather than a single answer.

The three free platforms

  • Claude — the thinking and creating. Ideas, writing, products, sales copy, customer replies.
  • Zapier — connecting your tools and automating the admin between them.
  • Taskade — project management and your weekly workflow.

What you will have by the end

  • 4 Claude agents installed as permanent one-word skills.
  • 4 or more Zapier automations running automatically without you.
  • A complete Taskade workspace with 2 agents running your Monday morning routine.

What to have ready before starting

A free Claude account at claude.ai, a free Zapier account at zapier.com, a free Taskade account at taskade.com, and a Google account with access to Google Forms, Gmail, Sheets and Docs.

That is the entire list. There is nothing to buy at any point in this course, and no free trial that quietly turns into a subscription in thirty days.

Section 4 — How to Use the Output

Work through the modules in order — Claude first, then Zapier, then Taskade — because each one builds on the one before it.

Your Claude agents produce the work. Your Zaps feed those agents with leads and ideas automatically. Taskade is where all of it lands and gets scheduled. Skip ahead to Module 3 and you will be organising work that does not exist yet.

Each lesson has a prompt to run and an action to complete before moving on. Do not skip the actions. They build the foundation the next lesson assumes you already have.

If you get stuck on any prompt, re-read the lesson and check that every bracket has been replaced with your real details.

This is the single biggest difference between a student who gets brilliant results and one who gets bland ones. Vague brackets produce vague outputs. Specific real details produce specific useful results.
